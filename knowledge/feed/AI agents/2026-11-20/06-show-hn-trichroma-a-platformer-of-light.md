Hi Hackernews! I just wanted to share a game I've made over a few weeks just as a little side project. Of course it was developed using Agents Engineering. The idea of the game though comes from around 10 years ago, from conversations with my brother: he is an artist and back them he was experimenting with chromatic paintings that looked differently depending on which color of light they were exposed to. A few weeks ago I decided to show my brother what's possible now a days with AI and how we can turn ideas into reality with a fraction of the effort we would have need in the past. The game is a little platformer, meant to be challenging but cute and entertaining, taking inspiration of games like Celeste or Meatboy. Death is part of the game, and light mechanics are introduced over the course of the 63 levels that are available to play. The development was made fully from my phone with Claude Code, but I invested lots of time on play testing it and I've involved several friends to play it to gather feedback and improve it. I'd love to hear your feedback and I hope some of you have fun playing it!
