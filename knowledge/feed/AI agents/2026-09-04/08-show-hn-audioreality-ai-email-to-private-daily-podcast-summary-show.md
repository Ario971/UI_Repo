Hey all, TLDR: When you sign up you get an email alias @audioreality.ai that you can use to sign up for newsletters, have agents send output to, etc. Once a day you get a private podcast with a multi-host format/conversation, so it’s not a basic summary and it’s not a verbatim readout. You can configure multiple shows too, so I personally have some based on different subjects I’m interested in. I built this because I have much more opportunity to listen to content than sit and read (exercising, commuting, background noise), but I find a lot of really valuable newsletters out there. So I’ve been trying to dial in a tolerable AI-generated format to get the info out in a way that’s easy for me to consume. This opened a few adjacent use cases like having scheduled agents email research, Google News style updates, etc. (even top HN feed stuff). I’d love feedback on the format approach, the script quality, “host” behaviors/personalities, and anything else. The audio quality is a constant work in progress as I test different audio models (and I completely understand if audio AI totally creeps you out). Thanks!
