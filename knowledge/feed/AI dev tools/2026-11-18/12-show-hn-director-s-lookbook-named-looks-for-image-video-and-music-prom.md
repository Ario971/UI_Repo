River Road Labs is a small artisanal studio based out of Central NJ. We built this tool to scratch our own itch. As AI video and image capabilities get more sophisticated we found that we struggled at times to find the words to describe the aesthetic style we were shooting (no pun) for. Other times it is good to just have a massive resource of styles and imagery to flip through at random and to let serendipity do the rest. Director's Lookbook lets you type a feeling and get dealt a set of style cards with prompt text to use. No more copying or saving prompts, use the tool. The feeling reader is a small hosted model with a free daily allowance. The MCP server is read only, no auth, no key, it works in Claude as a connector and in Cursor as well. Setup is at directorslookbook.app/mcp. The connector keeps no request text, only daily counts of which tools were called. The tool is free, no login, no account. We welcome feedback on how to make the tool better and what looks are missing! Thanks!
