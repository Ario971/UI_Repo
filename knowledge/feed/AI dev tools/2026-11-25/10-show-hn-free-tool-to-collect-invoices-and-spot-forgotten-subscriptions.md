At my previous company, I spent hours every month going through our bank statements and looking for the invoice behind each charge. There are many invoice collectors, with varying accuracy, but AI makes this much better now. I also noticed that while checking the statements, I was subconsciously reviewing the tools we pay for. So I built a free tool that works exactly how I wanted it to. It combines invoice collection with spotting forgotten subscriptions and duplicates, alerts for price rises and renewals, and some nifty actions, like drafting an email to ask for a discount. Many times it works, you just need to ask. It's fully free, but limited to reading 100 invoices a month, since I cover the AI costs and make no money from it. I also want to release a self-hosted version, where you could use your own models, including local ones. The code needs some cleanup and refactoring first, so I'll do it if people actually use this (I set the goal at 100 users).
