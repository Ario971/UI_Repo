Hi all, I switched from wallabag to readeck and was missing a way to sync articles to my kobo device, which was previously done by wallabako[1]. I forked that project, discarded some features (support for KOReader/Plato, on-screen progress etc.), extended testing and documentation to end up with Kobodeck, which after some months of use feels stable enough to share. This could be useful for people who only want to read articles on their kobo, there is at least one more full-featured solution being developed that can do much more than that[2]. Happy to receive any feedback or suggestions about this. [1] https://gitlab.com/anarcat/wallabako [2] https://bandarlabs.github.io/Cobalt/
