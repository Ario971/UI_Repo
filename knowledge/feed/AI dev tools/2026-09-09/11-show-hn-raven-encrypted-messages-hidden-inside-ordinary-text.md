Solo dev. built in my spare time. Raven uses a combination of steganography and cryptography to create a metadata resistant messenger. It encrypts any piece of text on your device and stores the ciphertext on Solana. It works pairwise with keys, users decrypt locally on their device- no accounts. I do run the relayer and a retention sweeper (not claiming peer to peer). One more thing, every write is signed by the same pool wallet, so anyone can see volume/timing. Check the white paper below and there’s been no audit. Several variations include: public, private, and stealth (for fun, i’ve hidden a public raven in this post) White Paper: https://oara.ai/raven-whitepaper The crypto and encoding core is open source.
