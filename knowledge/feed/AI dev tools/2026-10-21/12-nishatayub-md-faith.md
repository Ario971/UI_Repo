nishatayub/md-faith
MD-Faith is an evaluation harness, not another MD agent (MDCrow, DynaMate and others already run MD workflows). It computes ground truth from a trajectory, plants known processing problems (periodic-boundary wrapping, shuffled frames, unfitted frames), asks LLMs to explain the analysis under different conditions.
