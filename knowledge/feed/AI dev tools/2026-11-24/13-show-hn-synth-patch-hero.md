I wanted to be more deliberate and systematic in how I approach working on synth patches. With the help of our friend Claude, I put together a game that runs through exercises that builds that muscle in a fun and approachable way where you learn as you build patches on a modular rack. I do not have a Eurorack, nor a strong desire to own one (yet); but I do have a bit of other physical gear. I wanted to see if the lessons would translate to boxes, where sound is also better than the browser. So I have started adding groove box specific enhanced lessons (so far just a couple for the Digitone II, but they worked so far). Also: - You can set up MIDI in settings and play your patch in the browser with a MIDI keyboard (Chrome worked, but Safari didn't when I tried). - The Help in the exercises can unblock you when you get stuck and teach you as it helps. - You can pick a groove box in settings under 'my gear' to get some device-specific lessons.
