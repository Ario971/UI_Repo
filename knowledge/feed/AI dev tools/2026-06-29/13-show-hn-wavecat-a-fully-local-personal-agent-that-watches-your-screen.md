wavecat is a fully local personal agent that watches your screen. It develops a rich understanding of your needs and goals by constantly viewing your activity. Don't worry, none of your personal data ever leaves your computer since all the models run locally. I think it's something cool to show a feature that local agent systems can do that cloud systems can't. If you have a beefy Apple Silicon Mac or a nice GPU, you should be able to run it!
