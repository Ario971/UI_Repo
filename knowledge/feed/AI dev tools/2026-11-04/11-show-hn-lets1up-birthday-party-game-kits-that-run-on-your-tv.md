Hey HN parents out there, I started making party kits for my kids birthday parties and get togethers and wanted to share it with other device-less families out there. Ironically, its a web-app to use your TV to facilitate device-less team building and face-to-face time. I've ran the games at our home a couple of times and its been a success, hence the more games. Would love your feedback, you can use the code PKFREE to try it out. totally no charge. I got a few game packs setup Nerf, Minute-it to win-it, and werewolf. Disclaimer, I did use heavy AI assist to develop it all but why not use the tech to make our lives more connected? I hope it brings your family and friends more fun and time with each other.
