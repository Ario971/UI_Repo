I have been using some third part tools for the purpose of cutting out the silences and filler words from my amateur podcasts and wanted to give it a shot myself to build one that works on the browser. Ad-free, install-free and account-free version. And here is the result. It works completely on browser and has zero backend api connections. Everything stays in your browser. Chrome or edge needed though. Analyzes 2h of video in 10 seconds on my mac air :) So pretty fast. You have option to export it on the browser or download timeline for final cut, premiere pro, davinci resolve or capcut. Feel free to use, create PR's or give feedback.
