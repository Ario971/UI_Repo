I was buying a Mac mini and kept going back and forth between configs, trying to guess which one would actually run the models I want. So I built a configurator for people buying a Mac to run local LLMs: https://modelfit.io/ You can clearly see all the best llms it can read and at what efficiency all updating in real time according to the configuration you pick. You pick a Mac and a config, and it shows which models fit in memory and an estimated speed for each. The speed estimates come from a model fitted on real llama-bench runs, and every number shows its typical error, so you know how much to trust it. Apple list prices update daily. So I built my own configurator especially for people like me that are purchasing a mac in order to do some local ai. It's my own project. I'd love feedback, especially from people who can compare the estimates with their own numbers.
