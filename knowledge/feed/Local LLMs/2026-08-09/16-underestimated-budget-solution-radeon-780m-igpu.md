There are so many posts where people complaining about high prices and asking for solution So, there is one solution to consider: PC/mini PC/laptop on Ryzen 7 260 / Ryzen 9 8945HX /etc CPU with 780m iGPU and 64 Gb of DDR5 RAM. Barebone mini PC costs around 300-400, used 2x 32Gb DDR5 SO-DIMM around 500, used SSD 50-100 in my area. Here are my numbers on Ryzen 7 260, Ubuntu 26 with kernel params amdgpu.gttsize=49152 amd_iommu=off ttm.pages_limit=16777216 (48Gb of "VRAM") and llama.cpp with Vulkan. All LLMs are Unsloth Q8 quants.
