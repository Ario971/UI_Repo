CUDA: fix ROCm CI, do not pick the tile FA kernel for DKQ 576 with a GQA ratio not divisible by 4 (#33045) * CUDA: flash attention tile kernel for DKQ 576 with a GQA ratio of 2 The tile kernel for 576/512 only had variants for GQA ratios that are multiples of 4, while the kernel selection accepts any ratio from 2, so an MLA attention with a ratio of 2 aborted on GPUs that use the tile kernel, such as RDNA. Add the 2 column configuration, copied from the 512/512 one of each table, and dispatch even ratios to it. Odd ratios have no tile variant: the selection now reports them as unsupported instead of picking the tile kernel. Cover the ratios 2 and 3 in test-backend-ops. * CUDA: pass the K/V types to the tile kernel for 576 with a GQA ratio of 2 #32901 added the K and V types as template parameters of the tile kernel, the ratio 2 dispatch for DKQ 576 still used the old parameter list. * CUDA: report DKQ 576 with a GQA ratio not divisible by 4 as unsupported by the tile kernel Use the selection instead of a new tile specialization, as suggested in review: drop the 2 column variant and only pick the tile kernel for 576/512 when the GQA ratio is a multiple of 4, the ncols2 = 4 and 16 variants it has.
