One more great model from Liquid AI! Good for edge: LFM3-Audio-2B is a compact, fully featured audio model that runs locally on laptops and edge devices . It takes text or audio as input and responds with text, speech, or both. Voice chat: ask questions aloud and receive streaming replies in text and speech. For text-only responses, the model can reason before answering. Multilingual speech: transcribe, synthesize, and translate speech across 18 languages. Audio understanding and reasoning: interpret recordings and answer questions about the speech, sounds, and events they contain. Tool calling: turn spoken requests into tool calls to control apps and devices, with text or speech alongside the calls.
