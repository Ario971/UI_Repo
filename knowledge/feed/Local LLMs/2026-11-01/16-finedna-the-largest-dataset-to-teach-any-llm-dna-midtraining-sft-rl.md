Hi everyone, we're releasing FineDNA, all the data we used to turn Qwen3-14B into Carbon-X, the strongest open language model on DNA. It contains: Midtraining: 300B+ tokens of genomes and DNA-rich text SFT: ~200K conversations RL: ~98K verifiable prompts Code and recipe are open too, so you can apply it to other LLMs. Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceBio/FineDNA Code: https://github.com/huggingface/carbon submitted by /u/loubnabnl [link] [comments]
