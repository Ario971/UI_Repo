With all of this fear mongering on the “dangers” of LLM’s, I see how using people’s ignorance and emotions on the subject can be used as a tool to limit and restrict the tool from the average person. My fear is that China and the US https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-china-should-stay-clear-eyed-ai-cooperation-competition-bessent-says-2026-11-11/ will use people’s lack of understanding of local models to push for controlled intelligence. By implementing regulation on local AI, I can see how the US and China would want to force the average person to only be allowed to use models hosted on state monitored hardware. This way they can spy on what everyone is using hosted models for, limit what we use LLM’s for, and monitor the information they give us. People are being mislead to believe that people like us could use local models to create WMDs and attack tech infrastructure across the globe. But when you think deeply about it, you’ll realize that knowledge on a subject does not equal the means to carry out the subject. In order for someone to create a bio weapon, they would have to have access to a bio lab and the technology needed to create such a weapon. I know how a nuclear reactor works. Doesn’t mean I can make one in my backyard. I’m scared that arguably the greatest technology to come out of the 21st century could be limited and used to control us. Local LLM research MUST continue and the tech must be available for the common person. Be aware of future regulation. submitted by /u/New-Pressure-6932 [link] [comments]
