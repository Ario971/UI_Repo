Hey r/LocalLLaMA ! I made this quick demo of how Observer can run local models to monitor anything with your phone! These are some crazy things I've monitored: When someone that's not me, approaches my car, send me a picture. When a bird is on the bird's nest, send me a telegram with the picture. When my washing machine is on the rinse cycle, call me. When my dog finishes eating their food, notify me. When a machine at the gym is available, call me. So pretty much anything! I love seeing what you guys monitor. What would you use it for? (So I can make the agent better) Which models run on the phone? The default models are OneJev-0.8b for decision models and qwen-0.8b for LLM watchers. These run even on old phones which you may have laying around somewhere. Better to have them monitoring something than collecting dust :DD Try it out! It's free and OSS GitHub: https://github.com/Roy3838/Observer iOS: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/observer-ai/id6758222050 Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.observer.ai Btw I know my camera quality is kinda bad hahaha I wanted to ship the demo as soon as possible so you guys can see it. I'm here for any questions and feedback! Roy submitted by /u/Roy3838 [link] [comments]
