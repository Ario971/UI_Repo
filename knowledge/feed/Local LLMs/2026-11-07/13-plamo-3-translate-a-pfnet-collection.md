Overview We have released the weights for PLaMo 3 Translate , our translation-specialized LLM, on Hugging Face. The models come in four sizes—0.8B, 2B, 8B, and 31B. Even the smallest 0.8B model outperforms existing translation-specialized LLMs, including models with more than 30 times as many parameters, in Japanese–English translation and translation between Japanese and other languages . The key was meticulous data refinement through techniques such as tournament-based data synthesis, back-translation, and iterative self-distillation, combined with distillation into smaller models. In this post, we describe how we developed these models and how they perform. Released Model Weights PLaMo 3 Translate comes in four model sizes: 0.8B, 2B, 8B, and 31B . All four are available on Hugging Face . For each size, we provide BF16 weights, several quantized variants, and models in GGUF and MLX formats. Choose the version that best suits your environment. We quantized the models using tools such as AutoRound [2]. In our benchmarks, all of the quantization formats retained nearly the same accuracy as the BF16 versions. See the model cards for details. PLaMo 3 Translate supports translation across 53 languages . It accepts surrounding context and glossaries, and lets users choose between plain and polite Japanese. For detailed usage instructions, see the individual model cards on Hugging Face. PLaMo 3 Translate is released under the PLaMo Community License . For private purposes such as personal study and hobbies, you can download the models free of charge and use them freely. Noncommercial use, including academic use, has no income or revenue limit and requires no prior registration with PFN. Commercial use is also available free of charge if the user or its affiliates had income or revenue of JPY 1 billion or less in the most recent fiscal year, provided that the user registers in advance through the dedicated form . Commercial use above this threshold requires a separate license agreement; please contact PFN .
