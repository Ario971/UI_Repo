Hey r/LocalLLaMA ! We're releasing new new Unsloth Dynamic v3.1 GGUFs for Qwen3.8-27B! They're better by 5-10% accuracy on DeepSWE, Terminal Bench and more vs UD3, GSQ-RCO and Exllama-3! We're also improving our algorithms further so we can apply them to Qwen4 so folks can have efficient and accurate quants! All quants from 2-bit until 6-bit are updated. 3-bit ones are not, as we found UD3 to do on par with UD3.1. We are still improving all other quant sizes though! All benchmarks are 256K for agentic ones (DeepSWE, Terminal Bench), and 128K for the rest (LiveCodeBench etc). We used temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, min_p 0, no presence or repetition penalty, preserve_thinking and xhigh reasoning We ran benchmarks multiple times including LiveCodeBench x6 times, HMMT and others 8-16x and agentic benchmarks multiple times. On other benchmarks like for GSQ-RCO and Exllama3: To compare GSQ-RCO and Exllama-v3, we had to exclude the token embedding layer from the x axis, as Exllama-v3 uses BF16 for it. If you consider the full download size and total RAM usage for Exllama-v3, then adding the BF16 embedding table and including all embedding sizes changes the plots. Our main plots's x axis removes the embedding layer sizes from all quant providers. However on pure RAM usage and disk space usage vs accuracy, exllama3 does worse. For LiveCodeBench, GSQ-RCO showed Unsloth UD3 quants doing consistently worse than ISTA's. However we found the opposite - UD3 quants (our OLD quants) do better than ISTA on LCB on all sizes except the smallest. UD3.1 further improves on LCB as well. We approximate that GSQ-RCO used a context budget of 96K, as that best fits the data for the runs we have. At 96K, GSQ-RCO does somewhat do better than UD3, but at 128K, UD3 does much better. On another benchmark, for ISTA GSQ-RCO's AIME25 benchmarks, their quants reach 100%, 100%, 100% and 96.7% accuracy recovery. And they said our quants do worse. Through our investigations, we estimate ISTA only did 1 trial - we did 14 trials, and we show on average the recovery rate is 93% to 96% - not 100% accuracy recovery. And in fact our UD3 old quants do better on average than their quants on 14 runs vs 1 run of theirs, so we draw the opposite conclusion if you run the trials more. More details and benchmarks at https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.8#dynamic-v3.1-update-november-11th-2026 All quants are updated in https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF ! Thank you and have a nice day! We're super excited for Qwen4! submitted by /u/danielhanchen [link] [comments]
