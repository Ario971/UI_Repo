Now that it has vision, DeepSeek-V4-Flash is able to take game screenshots, allowing it to: Generate and correct game models and textures until they look right Fix any visual artifacts or glitches Write scripts to take sequences of screenshots for animations and correct animations Generally play-test the game, including UI and game mechanics The results are incredible, I was able to create a compelling game world in just a couple of days!
