Introduction Sage S2.1 Flash is a scientific multimodal model from Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory designed for research tasks that require multiple rounds of analysis and verification. It combines scientific data understanding with information retrieval, programming, and tool use. It can use specialized software to perform analyses, check results against intermediate outputs and visual feedback, and adjust subsequent steps accordingly. Through the unified representation architecture of Sage SciPerception , the model supports more than ten types of scientific input, including molecular graphs, crystal structures, and protein sequences and 3D structures. Features from each input type are encoded and projected into a shared representation space for reasoning alongside natural language instructions. These inputs support tasks such as prediction, classification, site localization, quantitative analysis, and structure inference.
