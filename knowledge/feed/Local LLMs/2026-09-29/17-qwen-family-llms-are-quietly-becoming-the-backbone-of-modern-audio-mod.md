I started mapping the building blocks shared across all the models in audio.cpp. The result ended up being more interesting than I expected. Qwen has become by far the most common language backbone in this collection: 32 audio model families use a Qwen-family architecture, and 20 of them use Qwen3 LLM specifically. And it’s no longer just TTS. Qwen-based models now show up across speech synthesis, ASR/audio understanding, music generation, speech-to-speech, and even audio/video models.
